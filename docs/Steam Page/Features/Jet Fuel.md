# Jet fuel

## Steam copy draft

Jet aircraft need jet fuel. Refine three units of chemfuel into one unit of jet fuel at the biofuel refinery, then load it into the Warthog. Jet fuel is a separate resource and is not accepted by standard chemfuel generators.

## Image brief

Illustrate the simple 3:1 conversion at a biofuel refinery: three chemfuel containers flowing into one jet-fuel container, then a clear arrow to the Warthog's fuel tank. Make the distinction between chemfuel and jet fuel obvious by color and label.

## Current implementation facts

- Recipe: 3 chemfuel produces 1 jet fuel.
- Crafted at a biofuel refinery after Biofuel Refining research.
- Market value is 6.9, matching three units of chemfuel at the current base-game value.
- The Warthog uses jet fuel and does not use ordinary chemfuel.
- The item definition does not add a generator that burns jet fuel.
