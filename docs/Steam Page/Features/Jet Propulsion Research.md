# Jet propulsion research and production

## Steam copy draft

Jet propulsion branches from Aerial Vehicles in the Vehicles research tree. Complete the research and its required techprint to unlock the specialized parts used to manufacture the Warthog. The production chain gives the aircraft its own engine, intakes, exhausts, fuel tanks, and general aircraft parts.

## Image brief

Show the Vehicles research tree with Aerial Vehicles leading into Jet Propulsion, and use a second panel for the aircraft production chain: research → jet parts → Warthog. Make the techprint requirement visible without implying that the project has more prerequisites than it currently does.

## Current implementation facts

- Research project: Jet Propulsion.
- Prerequisite: Aerial Vehicles.
- Current research cost: 2,000.
- Requires one techprint.
- Unlocks crafting for the jet-specific components and is a prerequisite for the Warthog build.
- The research is in the Vehicles tab.
