# Jet parts

## Steam copy draft

Jet parts represent the many specialized assemblies needed to build and maintain aircraft, including control electronics, actuators, wiring, valves, fittings, and precision hardware. They provide a general-purpose aircraft component for the Warthog's construction recipe.

## Image brief

Create a compact assortment of varied aerospace hardware in one readable icon: wiring, a small actuator, a control module, fittings, and fasteners. Keep the silhouette distinct from vanilla components and from the jet engine parts.

## Current recipe and stats

- Cost: 10 steel, 2 silver, 2 gold, and 3 industrial components.
- Work: 3,000. Market value: 149. Mass: 4.
- Requires Jet Propulsion research; crafted through the VVE vehicle-part production system.
- The Warthog build currently requires 15.
