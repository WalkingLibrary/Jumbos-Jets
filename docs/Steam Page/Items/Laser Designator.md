# Laser designator

## Steam copy draft

The laser designator is an equippable tool for calling in more accurate close air support. A drafted pawn can aim at a target and hold the designation while remaining stationary and keeping a clear line of sight. The Warthog can use the mark to guide a strafing run that passes nearby.

## Image brief

Create a clean item render of a handheld military laser designator, with a visible optic and emitter. Pair the item image with a gameplay panel showing a pawn aiming a bright green beam. Keep the coverage radius and attack-line interaction in the separate Laser Guidance feature graphic.

## Current item facts

- Crafted at a machining table from 1 steel and 1 industrial component.
- No research prerequisite.
- Current range: 120 cells; charges: 25; market value: 33.9.
- The pawn must be drafted, stationary, and have line of sight. A move order cancels the designation.
- The beam and guidance preview are green.
