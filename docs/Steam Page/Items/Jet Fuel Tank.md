# Jet fuel tank

## Steam copy draft

The jet fuel tank stores and feeds the specialized fuel required by the Warthog. It takes refined jet fuel to manufacture, tying aircraft construction to the fuel supply chain.

## Image brief

Show a reinforced aircraft tank with clearly visible fittings or a feed line. Include a small jet-fuel canister beside it to signal its dedicated fuel type.

## Current recipe and stats

- Cost: 10 steel, 30 jet fuel, and 2 silver.
- Work: 2,000. Market value: 97. Mass: 10.
- Requires Jet Propulsion research; crafted through the VVE vehicle-part production system.
- The Warthog build requires two tanks.
