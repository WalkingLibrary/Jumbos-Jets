# Jet engine

## Steam copy draft

The jet engine is the Warthog's primary propulsion component. Its reinforced construction and specialized materials make it substantially more demanding to manufacture than a standard vehicle engine.

## Image brief

Show the engine by itself on a neutral inventory-style background, then add a small scale reference or inset showing how two engines fit into the Warthog. Keep the engine's silhouette and major details clear enough to read at item-icon size.

## Current recipe and stats

- Cost: 80 steel, 8 silver, 8 gold, and 12 industrial components.
- Work: 6,000.
- Market value: 650; mass: 18.
- Crafted through the VVE vehicle-part production system after Jet Propulsion research.
