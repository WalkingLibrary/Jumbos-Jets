[h1]Jumbos Jets: Close Air Support for RimWorld[/h1]

Build, crew, and send the Warthog into action. Jumbos Jets brings heavy close air support to RimWorld, from researching jet propulsion and manufacturing aircraft parts to keeping your plane fueled and on station. Draw a strafing line across an existing map, spend steel to unleash the run, and use a laser designator to guide its accuracy.

[h2]What's inside[/h2]

Take command of the Warthog, a purpose-built attack jet. Develop its specialized engines and components, produce dedicated jet fuel, then launch aerial sorties over maps in your world. Strafing runs strike along a line you choose, while a pawn using the laser designator can help focus the attack.

[h2]A note about the mod[/h2]

Jumbos Jets is a work in progress, and its balance, systems, and compatibility may still have rough edges. Bug reports must include the full [b]Player.log[/b] from the session where the issue occurred, steps to reproduce it, and your game and mod versions. Reports without session logs will not be reviewed.

[h3]Finding your log[/h3]

Get the log after the problem occurs and before relaunching RimWorld; the game replaces it when it starts.

[list]
[*][b]Windows:[/b] Press Win+R, enter %localappdata%low\Ludeon Studios\RimWorld by Ludeon Studios, and press Enter. Attach Player.log from that folder.
[*][b]macOS:[/b] In Finder, press Cmd+Shift+G, enter ~/Library/Logs/Ludeon Studios/RimWorld by Ludeon Studios/, and press Enter. Attach Player.log from that folder.
[/list]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]

[b]How do I unlock the Warthog and its parts?[/b] Research Jet Propulsion in the Vehicles tree. It branches from Aerial Vehicles and requires a techprint.

[b]How do I make jet fuel?[/b] Refine three chemfuel into one jet fuel at a biofuel refinery after researching Biofuel Refining.

[b]Can the Warthog attack a map it has not visited?[/b] It can attack an existing map associated with its current world location; a sortie does not generate a new map.

[b]Does the laser designator deal damage?[/b] No. It marks a target and improves the accuracy of a Warthog run that passes close to the designation.

[b]Can a pawn move while using the laser designator?[/b] A move order cancels the designation and lets the pawn move.

[b]Are other aircraft and air defenses included?[/b] The current release includes the Warthog. Other aircraft, anti-air weapons, and enemy air support are future ideas.
